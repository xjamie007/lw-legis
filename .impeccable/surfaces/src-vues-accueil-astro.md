# Surface brief: public website (home and inner pages)

Scope: every public page of the LW LEGIS site. Visitor mode: Persuade on the home page, Read on activities, files, conferences, lawyers and legal pages.

Audience and job: an entrepreneur, investor or creditor with a concrete Luxembourg matter checks whether the firm handles it, then calls or writes. Proof is the firm's own record: Me Lorang since 1982, his credentials, the networks, the published files and conference papers. Constraint: content from the old site only, FR and EN, nothing invented.

Chosen: the category standard (user choice), executed at the craft of Elvinger Hoss Prussen, Arendt & Medernach and Bredin Prat.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A classic Luxembourg law-firm site done with the restraint of the top firms: white pages, deep navy, the firm's gold arms, real photographs, set with care. It refuses icon cards, stock imagery, eyebrows above headings and template filler.

OWN-WORLD: White ground; navy #13294B owns the header band, the hero, the network band and the footer; ink #0E1A2B; gold #A88A57 from the arms only as rules, the logo and text on navy (#C9AE7C). Spectral for headings at light weight and large size, Hanken Grotesk for text and interface. Hairline rules, no shadows on content, square corners.

STORY: The visitor sees a real firm (name, arms, photo, address, since 1982), finds their matter in the activities index, trusts the record (credentials, networks, files, papers) and calls or writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy hero across the full width: left, the headline "Cabinet d'avocats au Luxembourg" in Spectral at about 5rem, the firm's one-sentence specialisation below, then two actions: "Prendre rendez-vous" (gold-ruled button) and the phone number as a live link. Right, the office photograph cropped tall, edge to edge. Above it a white header with the arms, the navigation and FR | EN.

FORM: The category standard (canon exit), rank n/a; seed key 9d394df4.

SIGNATURE: The activities index: 13 matters in two columns, each a ruled row whose title slides right and gains a gold rule on hover; on each activity page a sticky side index of all matters and a "Contacter l'Étude à ce sujet" box that pre-fills the form. Motion: one gentle reveal of the hero text, nothing scattered.
